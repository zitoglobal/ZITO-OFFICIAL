Capabilities

From brief to sealed bulk

Three capabilities in one group. The brand you brief depends on the category — so sample and bulk stay with the desk that understands the product.

01

Style design

Silhouette, block, pattern and fit developed from a brief or a reference. Branding placement, trims and construction details are decided with the sample — before bulk is cut.

  • Concept to pattern and grading
  • Fit sessions and revision cycles
  • Branding and trim decisions locked with the sample
02

Fabric development

Hand-feel, stretch, recovery, weight and colour matched to the garment. Mill work is tied to the style — not bought as a generic stock story.

  • Performance knits and swim cloth for ZIESHINE and ELEVIA
  • Fashion wovens, knits and soft-tailoring cloth for MANLOR
  • Colour, hand-feel and performance targets agreed before bulk
03

Manufacturing

Sampling and fit come first. Bulk follows once the seal is agreed. Production stays with the brand that owns that category — so the seal is not reinterpreted by a different team.

  • Sample → seal → controlled bulk
  • QC points tied to the approved reference
  • Category desk owns the run from fit to delivery

Where to brief

ZIESHINE — activewear & swim · ELEVIA — men’s performance · MANLOR — fashion · Or contact the group when the programme spans design, fabric and production across more than one lane.
