About

Beyond Borders. Beyond Business.

ZITO GLOBAL is the group front door. Design, fabric and manufacturing stay connected — while each brand owns its category lane.

How the group is set

Guangzhou Zito Global Co., Ltd. does not fold every category into one generic factory story. ZIESHINE, ELEVIA and MANLOR each keep a clear product lane and their own site. ZITO holds the group path from brief to sealed bulk.

That structure keeps the brief sharp: the category brand owns the sample and the sealed bulk, while the group keeps style, cloth and production connected when a programme needs more than one step.

Integrated work

A programme can start at style, at cloth, or at a sealed sample. The same group can carry it through to bulk without handing the brief to a stranger.

Separate lanes

Activewear and swim. Men’s performance. Contemporary fashion. The brief goes to the brand built for that category — not a catch-all desk.

What we sell

Not a catalogue of SKUs. A controlled path: design decisions, fabric platforms and manufacturing that can be sealed and repeated.
